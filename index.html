      About this tool

      A study of leanSPHINCS, a hash-based post-quantum signature for Ethereum accounts: how its size in bytes and the Keccak permutations to verify it move throughput in twelve deployment scenarios. Nothing here was measured on a real network, except the receipts under "Measured on chain"; one Ledger run of the working set is the only device measurement.

      • measured Forge gas or a receipt
      • counted leanVM row counts
      • simulated network simulator
      • modelled EIP text or a timing model
      What is measured, counted, simulated and modelled
      LevelWhat it coversHow it was obtained
      measuredEVM verification gas of the five single-layer sets. The receipts under "Measured on chain" in Explore.Foundry tests on a real Solidity verifier, 52 test vectors per set. The worst-case figure is a model fitted on those measurements. The receipts are Safe-module and EIP-7702 transactions on a local anvil chain with that verifier, and transactions of other parameter sets of the family on Sepolia and on the ethrex EIP-8141 testnet, as the SPHINCs- repository records them.
      countedleanVM cycles and Keccak rows per signature.Exact row counts from leanVM proof runs. They are counts, not timings.
      simulatedThroughput, what binds, block size, block reach times, bandwidth and duplicates in the tables and in browser runs.Counts from the network simulator: 40 nodes and three seeds for most table cells. Its inputs (gas, prover prices, uplinks) are modelled.
      modelledGas per transaction, prover prices, uplink rates, Ledger signing and key generation times, lifetimes, the calculator, and the percentage columns of the tables without aggregation.Derived from EIP texts (several are drafts), from a prover model on the counted rows, and from a timing model at 5,800 permutations per second, a third-party benchmark rate.
      • One set was measured on a Ledger. The working set's signer ran on a Nano S+ (50 signatures and one key generation; the figures are under "Choose a set"). Every other set's signing time is a permutation count divided by a benchmark rate.
      • Nothing in the tables or the simulator was measured on a real network. No client, devnet or testnet was involved in the campaign, and no EIP-8141 transaction has been run with the campaign's verifiers. The receipts under "Measured on chain" are the exception: they are real transactions, but of other parameter sets of the same family, deployed earlier and recorded in the SPHINCs- repository.
      • Throughput is a ceiling for one kind of transaction. A block of plain ECDSA transfers (21,000 gas each) at 60 M gas every 12 s holds at most 238.10 tx/s; every throughput figure here is of that kind, a gas bound or a simulated capacity. Mainnet really carries about 15 to 20 tx/s, because real transactions do more than a transfer and use more gas each.
      • Gas per transaction in Explore, in the calculator and in Simulate is the gas model's figure, split into fixed cost, calldata, verification and overhead. The build stops if the model and a recorded receipt differ by more than 1%.
      • Where gas binds, the simulation returns whole transactions per block from the same gas figures as the model. Agreement there is not independent evidence. The simulator adds information where the network or the provers bind.
      • Gas figures follow EIP-7623, EIP-7976, EIP-8141, EIP-8288 and the leanPCS draft as read on 10 November 2026. Several are drafts and may change. The price of "8288 light, raw signatures" is the campaign's own assumption.
      • Rows with more than one signature per transaction: EIP-8141's public mempool accepts one VERIFY frame for the sender and caps the declared validation gas at 100,000. Two signatures fit only if signature bytes do not count toward that limit, as a draft pull request proposes. A Safe-style multisig can check its owners' signatures in the execution frame instead. Neither design was run on a client.
      Limits of the simulator
      • It is a discrete-event model of gossip, block propagation and proving, not a client. Most table cells are 40-node runs. Uplink rates inside each class are modelled.
      • It sends eight full copies of each block where current clients send three or four. The loss it shows at 300 M gas is therefore an upper bound.
      • The capacity search is not monotone near a prover bound and depends on its start point by about 5%. It also accepts a probe whose chain has collapsed, because it ignores orphaned blocks.
      • The label for what binds reads propagation for a slow uplink class, and flips on a CPU threshold of 0.9.
      • It has no IDONTWANT, no separate setting for relay copies, no cap of 256 dependencies per block, and it does not re-queue orphaned transactions.
      • The first hop of the user-side-proof variant (F2) is not simulated: F2 runs F1's mempool under another label.
      • A misspelt candidate name on the command line runs today's set under the typo's label. This page only offers the names the simulator knows.

      This build, and privacy

      The simulator is a fork of cskiraly/stark-mempool-sim (netsim/v2) with named scenarios, per-node uplinks, block priority and explicit queue policies. The site is static and self-contained: it loads nothing from another origin, sets no cookie and sends nothing anywhere. The Simulate form is remembered in this browser's local storage; nothing else is stored.

      The same thing from the command line

      The simulator needs Python 3 and nothing else. From the simulator checkout, on the leansphincs branch at the commit of this build:

      cd sims/stark-mempool-sim-v2
NP=../../data/phase2b/network_params_v2.json

# list the scenarios and their configurations
python3 -m bench.leansphincs scenarios

# one row of the tables: 8141 full, working set, 200 M gas, 40 nodes
python3 -m bench.leansphincs run --scenario A --candidate P99r \
    --gas-limit 200000000 --seed 0 --network-params $NP --phase2-row

# a custom point: 4,000 bytes and 300 permutations on the working set's verifier
python3 -m bench.leansphincs run --scenario A --candidate P99r \
    --sig-bytes 4000 --perms 300 --network-params $NP --phase2-row

      Every result in Simulate shows the exact line for that run. The table cells are means over three seeds (0, 1, 2) for most rows; a single run is one seed.
