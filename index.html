How to read this page

  • Capacity (each step's headline): what the simulated network includes when more post-quantum transactions are offered than fit, at that step's gas limit, with today's ECDSA traffic alongside. This is the result.
  • At an assumed demand (below it, secondary): the network at a load we assume (today's traffic grown a little each year, a PQ adoption ramp). Throughput there is the input echoed back; read only how full blocks are, latency and bandwidth.
  • Past the gas limit: what binds next when the gas limit is raised or removed, and the elasticities: how throughput moves with signature bytes and permutations.
  • Fees: gas per transaction and its ratios between deployments are solid; a fee in gwei is your base fee times that gas.
